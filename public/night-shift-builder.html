Place your tasks

Each player shift draws six random tasks across the rooms. You can still place reference stations here: choose a task and floor, then tap its location. Save map when finished.

Place decorations

Pick an item, then tap the map to place it. Drag, resize, rotate, or duplicate it afterward.

Save map publishes your map online for the whole game and all devices. Export a file for an extra backup.

Drag any hallway to move it, or use its corner to resize. Rooms and hallways start empty. Add jobs to either. New stairs let you choose their start and stop levels. Drag the elevator to place it. Each floor has its own elevator position. All elevator stops connect as one elevator. Add it on each floor you want it to serve. Clear floor removes everything on that floor, including elevator and stair stops. Select a room, add a doorway, then click the wall where you want it. Adjust its reach to bridge the gap to a hallway. Automatic room connections are off.

Select a room.

New stairs appear only on the floor where you add them. Matching PRA stairs connect to PRA on other floors, and PRB connects to PRB. Changing the starting or stopping level also adds stops in that range. Players can go up or down. Deleting removes only this floor’s stop. Drag it to set its position on this floor. Other floors keep their own positions.

Decorations are visual and do not block walking.

Doorways

Click a room wall to place a doorway. Reach extends the opening toward your hallway. Add as many entrances as you need.

Job stations

Station X/Y are percentages within the room or hallway. For curves, X is the position along the bend.
