# Recorded dice sounds

`dice-roll-1.wav`, `dice-roll-2.wav`, and `dice-roll-3.wav` are recordings from
**Casino Audio 1.1** by **Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)**.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- Original files: `Audio/dice-throw-1.ogg`, `Audio/dice-throw-2.ogg`, and `Audio/dice-throw-3.ogg`.
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The package's original license is preserved in `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
- Converted to mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV for consistent browser playback, including Safari.

The game varies the recorded throws across the dice animation. Playback changes do not affect game rolls.
