    How to solve the case

    1. Keep your cards private. Your joining name is your suspect name, and your role identifies your character. The killer, weapon, and room where the rookie died are hidden in the case file: one card from each category. The remaining cards are dealt to the detectives. Your cards rule those possibilities out.
    2. Explore the jail. Roll two dice and tap an outlined corridor square or room. Doorways are the only entrances. Entering a room ends your movement. You can use a corner staff passage instead of rolling.
    3. Spread a rumor. In a room, pick a suspect and weapon to spread a rumor about who killed the rookie there. The suspect and weapon move to that room. Spread only one rumor per turn; leave before spreading another rumor in the same room, unless another player summoned you there.
    4. Challenge the rumor. Starting with the next player, the first detective with a matching card shows you one privately. That evidence rules out part of the rumor. Detectives who made a wrong accusation still show cards.
    5. Separate rumors from the truth. Use your notebook to mark possibilities as ruled out, unsure, or likely. Cards in your own hand and cards shown to you are ruled out automatically.
    6. Make the final accusation. At the start or end of your turn, identify the killer, weapon, and room where the rookie died. Get all three right to win. You can name any room, regardless of where your pawn is. A wrong accusation ends your investigation. If everyone is out, the case file is revealed.

    Pass the device when prompted. Closing or refreshing the page covers the private cards again. This is a shared-device game.
