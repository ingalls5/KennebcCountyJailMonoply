    How to solve the case

    1. Keep your cards private. One suspect, one equipment item, and one room are hidden in the case file. The other 18 cards are dealt to the detectives. Your cards rule those possibilities out.
    2. Explore the jail. Roll two dice and tap an outlined corridor square or room. Doorways are the only entrances. Entering a room ends your movement. You can use a corner staff passage instead of rolling.
    3. Make a suggestion. In a room, pick a suspect and equipment item. The room you’re in completes the suggestion. The suspect and item move there. Suggest only once a turn; leave before suggesting in that same room again, unless another player summoned you there.
    4. Check the evidence. Starting with the next player, the first detective with a matching card shows you one privately. Detectives who made a wrong accusation still show cards.
    5. Use your notebook. Mark possibilities as ruled out, unsure, or likely. Cards in your own hand and cards shown to you are ruled out automatically.
    6. Make the final accusation. At the start or end of your turn, choose a suspect, item, and any room. Get all three right to win. A wrong accusation ends your investigation. If everyone is out, the case file is revealed.

    Pass the device when prompted. Closing or refreshing the page covers the private cards again. This is a shared-device game.
