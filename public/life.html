      THE COUNTY CAREER

      Build a life. Retire with a story.

      Start with $1,000. Choose the Academy for $500, reveal two unknown job cards at graduation, and choose one. Or collect $300 to start work and draw one of five unknown job cards. Play through your starting path, then join First Briefing. Spin 1–10 to move.

      Green Pay Days pay your salary when you pass or land on them. Your job card sets your salary, up to $1,500 for a Captain. Career Choice spaces offer three unknown job cards. Pick one and keep the job you reveal. Event spaces bring paychecks, expenses, marriage, children, and buying a home.

      Land on a blue LIFE space or choose an event that awards a tile to collect one face down. Each tile carries a hidden $100–$1,000 retirement bonus. Values are revealed only when everyone has retired. If the bank is empty, a tile can come from a retired player; if none are available, no tile is awarded.

      At Stop 56, choose the main road or the ten-space Mid-Life Crisis detour. The main road continues at Stop 57; the detour rejoins at Stop 67. Crisis decisions trade cash now against LIFE tiles and career opportunities. Passing the marked Pay Day at C5 pays your salary. Graduation, the end of your first shift, the path fork, and the final crisis space stop unused movement. Marriage, home buying, child, and divorce spaces are full stops; resolve the choice and spin again. Every required stop gives the same player another spin after all choices are finished, except retirement.

      You do not need an exact spin to retire. Sell your house when you retire; its sale value is paid into savings. After everyone retires, add savings, LIFE tile bonuses, and $100 per child. Debt reduces your total. The highest total wins; exact ties share the win.
