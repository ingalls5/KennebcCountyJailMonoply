      THE COUNTY CAREER

      Get to retirement. Keep a little life.

      First choose Go to the Academy ($500 cost, +3 reputation, Officer rank) or Start on the floor (+$300). Both use 1 energy. Roll one die, move along the path, and earn $200 shift pay plus $150 per rank above Rookie. Each turn uses 1 energy. Passing a promotion review offers a rank increase in exchange for reputation and energy. Other stops bring events, shift choices, or days off.

      Read the effects before choosing. Reputation and energy stay between 0 and 10. Reaching zero energy after a decision triggers $200 recovery and restores energy to 3. Savings can become debt.

      You do not need an exact roll to retire. Retired officers stop taking turns. Once the whole crew retires, savings + $100 per reputation + $50 per energy determines the winner. Exact ties share the win.
