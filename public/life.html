      THE COUNTY CAREER

      Get to retirement. Keep a little life.

      First choose Go to the Academy ($500 cost, 12 spaces) or Work in the Jail (+$300, 8 spaces). Both choices use 1 energy. Spin to move through your starting route and resolve the space you land on. You must stop at Graduation or First Shift Complete; unused movement ends there. Academy graduation gives you 3 reputation and Officer rank. After resolving the final starting space, join First Briefing and begin the main road on your next turn. Each spin (1–10) earns $200 shift pay plus $150 per rank above Rookie. Each turn uses 1 energy. Passing a promotion review offers a rank increase in exchange for reputation and energy. Other stops bring events, shift choices, or days off.

      At Stop 56, every career enters the ten-space Mid-Life Crisis branch once. Unused movement ends at the fork. If you passed a promotion review on the way, resolve it before the first crisis choice. Make the first crisis decision, then spin on later turns and face the dilemma you land on. You must stop at the last crisis space and make its choice before rejoining Stop 57; unused movement ends there too. Each option trades savings, reputation, or energy.

      Read the effects before choosing. Reputation and energy stay between 0 and 10. Reaching zero energy after a decision triggers $200 recovery and restores energy to 3. Savings can become debt.

      You do not need an exact spin to retire. Retired officers stop taking turns. Once the whole crew retires, savings + $100 per reputation + $50 per energy determines the winner. Exact ties share the win.
