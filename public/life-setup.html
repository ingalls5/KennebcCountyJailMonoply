How do you win?

Everyone begins with $1,000, 3 reputation, and 7 energy. First choose: go to the Academy for $500, gain 3 reputation and start at Officer rank, or start on the floor with an extra $300. Either route uses 1 energy. Then spin the wheel, collect shift pay, and make a decision. Passing a review gives you a chance to earn a promotion. Better ranks earn better pay.

After everyone retires, the highest total wins: savings + $100 per reputation + $50 per energy. Exact ties share the win. Negative savings count as debt. If a decision leaves you with zero energy, recovery costs $200 and restores energy to 3.

The amounts and ranks are game rules. Every career follows its own story.
