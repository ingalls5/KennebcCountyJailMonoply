How do you win?

Everyone begins with $1,000. Go to the Academy for $500 and graduate into Officer salary, or start work in the jail with an extra $300 and Rookie salary. Green Pay Days pay your salary when you pass or land on them. Career choices, expenses, marriage, children, and home purchases shape your story.

Collect face-down LIFE tiles from blue spaces and event choices. Each is worth $100–$1,000, revealed when everyone has retired. Your final total is savings + LIFE tile bonuses + home sale value + $100 per child. Debt reduces your total. The highest total wins; exact ties share the win.

At Stop 56, choose the main road or the ten-space Mid-Life Crisis detour, which rejoins at Stop 67. Its choices trade money now against LIFE tiles and career opportunities. The amounts are game rules, not county pay rates.

Clock in. Make your choices. Leave a legacy.
